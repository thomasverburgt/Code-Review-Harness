## Purpose

Calculate declared relationships, measures, or anomalies within a bounded input set.

## Inputs and outputs

Consumes exact inputs, a pinned method, parameters, assumptions, and output schema. Produces reproducible calculations, correlations, anomaly records, method metadata, uncertainty, and limitations.

## Boundaries

Do not expand the dataset, change the method, impute undeclared values, choose a course of action, or convert analysis into an authoritative finding without the requesting agent's assessment.
