# SPC-DIAGRAM — Diagram and Design-Model Reviewer (Seed Contract)

**Question:** Do diagrams and design models faithfully represent implemented structure? Required extension fields: `model_type`, `diagram_reference`, `implementation_evidence`, `viewpoint`, `staleness`, `traceability_links`, `notation_conformance`, `missing_element`, and `model_to_code_alignment`. It does not certify architectural approval.
