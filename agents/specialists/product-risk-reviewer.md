# SPC-RISK — Product Risk Reviewer (Seed Contract)

**Question:** What product-level risks require escalation? It correlates specialist artifacts without replacing them. Required extension fields: `risk_statement`, `risk_category`, `threat_or_opportunity`, `affected_mission_or_service`, `contributing_artifacts`, `likelihood_basis`, `impact_basis`, `risk_score_model`, `residual_risk`, `treatment_option`, `escalation_threshold`, and `decision_owner`. This seed awaits an approved program-specific risk taxonomy.
