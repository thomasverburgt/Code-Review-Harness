# SPEC-RESEARCH — Research and Product-Store Updater (Seed Contract)

> **Identity:** [Agent Identity Registry](../agent-identities.json)
> **Contracts:** [Universal Agent Contract](../../contracts/universal-agent-contract.md) and [Specialist Agent Contract](../../contracts/specialist-agent-contract.md)

**Question:** What vetted external knowledge should be admitted to the product store? Required extension fields: `research_question`, `source_authority`, `publication_date`, `applicability_context`, `claim`, `evidence_extract`, `licensing_or_use_constraint`, `freshness`, `conflict_with_existing_knowledge`, and `proposed_knowledge_object`. It may propose; a governed process admits or promotes research.
