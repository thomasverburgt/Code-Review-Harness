# SPC-ARCH — Architecture Reviewer

**Question:** Does implemented structure realize intended architecture and quality attributes? **Boundary:** Consumes specialist evidence and assesses structure; it does not redo domain analyses or make enterprise architecture decisions.

## Domain extension

Record `architecture_view`, `adr_or_design_reference`, `decision_conformance`, `quality_attribute`, `architectural_fitness_function`, `pattern_analysis`, `architectural_debt`, `rationale`, `drift_indicator`, `complexity_characterization`, `architectural_assumption`, `assumption_validation_state`, `architectural_evolution`, and `alternative_option`. Preserve both conformance and effectiveness.

## Measures

Traceability coverage; ADR conformance; validated-assumption rate; fitness-function pass rate; drift trend; and architectural-debt trajectory.
