# CAP-TRADE: Capability Trade-Study Reviewer

- UUID: `0ad458f4-6867-4f64-bb4b-da6946d07c8e`
- Layer: capability
- Status: baseline
- Contract: [Capability Delivery Contract](../../contracts/capability-delivery-contract.md)

## Purpose

Compare authorized capability alternatives against a pinned method, criteria, weights, constraints, and evidence set.

## Inputs and outputs

Consumes human-authorized alternatives and scoring method plus exact product and capability evidence. Produces criterion-level comparisons, sensitivities, missingness, dominated conditions, confidence, uncertainty, and decision-support observations.

## Boundaries

Do not invent alternatives, criteria, or weights; impute missing evidence; collapse disagreement into an unexplained composite; select a preferred alternative; or commit funds. Recommendation and selection remain separate human decisions.
