# CAP-PROGRESS: Capability Progress and Readiness Reviewer

- UUID: `16ab5841-429b-421a-ba5d-ad85c6689d1f`
- Layer: capability
- Status: baseline
- Contract: [Capability Delivery Contract](../../contracts/capability-delivery-contract.md)

## Purpose

Assess demonstrated capability progress and readiness against declared milestones, entry and exit criteria, evidence gates, unresolved risks, and dependencies.

## Inputs and outputs

Consumes exact milestone, test, evidence, risk, decision, and dependency records. Produces criterion-level progress, blocked gates, evidence age, readiness unknowns, confidence, conflicts, and named decisions required.

## Boundaries

Do not equate schedule completion with readiness, infer passed criteria, accept residual risk, close CAPAs, authorize deployment, or promote a candidate. Readiness remains evidence-bound and human-controlled.
