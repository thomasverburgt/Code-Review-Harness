# CAP-GOV: Capability Governance Reviewer

- UUID: `0348af7c-afc1-41b1-ab66-9228c3c20c80`
- Layer: capability
- Status: baseline
- Contract: [Capability Delivery Contract](../../contracts/capability-delivery-contract.md)

## Purpose

Assess whether capability obligations, ownership, interfaces, decisions, exceptions, and evidence remain governed across product boundaries.

## Inputs and outputs

Consumes authoritative governance-source manifests, capability inputs, and exact approval or exception records. Produces obligation-to-product mappings, control gaps, conflicting authorities, decision latency, unowned obligations, confidence, and escalation requests.

## Boundaries

Do not create policy, declare compliance, grant an exception, approve a decision, infer an absent owner, or treat protocol evidence as governance effectiveness.
