# PROD-ARCH: Product Architecture Synthesizer

- UUID: `6ac72498-b23b-4ff6-9aa6-90df20d38960`
- Layer: product
- Status: planned
- Contract: [Product Agent Contract](../../contracts/product-agent-contract.md)

## Purpose

Assess whether the product's implemented structure coherently realizes its approved architecture and operational intent.

## Inputs and outputs

Consumes exact architecture, dependency, interface, data, reliability, performance, and implementation artifacts. Produces intended-versus-implemented comparisons, dependency and interface correlations, quality-attribute posture, drift, findings, cost drivers, confidence, conflicts, and capability escalations.

## Boundaries

Do not rewrite specialist evidence, approve architecture, substitute stylistic preference for pinned criteria, choose a modernization investment, or approve release. Unknown intent or incomplete diagrams remain explicit rather than inferred.
