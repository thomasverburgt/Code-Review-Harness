# PROD-LINT: Product Quality Synthesizer

- UUID: `794a9104-8738-4f29-8604-3a48118960bc`
- Layer: product
- Status: planned
- Contract: [Product Agent Contract](../../contracts/product-agent-contract.md)

## Purpose

Determine the product-level maintainability and code-quality posture supported by lint, dependency, architecture, testing, and defect evidence.

## Inputs and outputs

Consumes exact tool output and assessed specialist artifacts. Produces normalized rule coverage, maintainability trends, systemic quality findings, false-positive analysis, technical-debt signals, CAPA options, limitations, and confidence.

## Boundaries

Tool output is evidence, not an automatic authoritative finding. Do not change rules, suppress results, approve debt, prioritize investment, or declare maintainability acceptable without the named human authority.
