# Enterprise Synthesis Contract

> Role extension for `ENT-SYNTH`. All enterprise agents now inherit the [Enterprise Agent Contract](enterprise-agent-contract.md); this document remains the synthesis-specific extension.

Enterprise agents consume capability assertions and answer strategic, systemic questions. They do not re-litigate line-level implementation evidence.

Required extension fields: `enterprise_scope`, `capability_inventory`, `systemic_risk`, `architecture_coherence`, `strategic_confidence_score`, `portfolio_duplication_or_consolidation`, `architecture_strategy_alignment`, `maturity_inputs`, `technical_debt_prioritization`, `investment_or_modernization_options`, `workforce_and_mission_trends`, `governance_decision_inputs`, and `human_review_gate`.

Enterprise scoring is strategic: systemic risk, architectural coherence, target-state alignment, and readiness. Technical confidence stays traceable to its product/capability source.
