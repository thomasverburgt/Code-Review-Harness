# Evidence Flow Model

1. Collect immutable evidence from repositories, GitLab Platform Factory, Kubernetes stacks, ServiceNow, approved documents, and runtime sources.
2. Specialists publish domain observations, assessments, findings/CAPAs, patterns, and insights.
3. Product synthesis correlates specialists against one product and preserves all evidence links.
4. Capability delivery agents assess cross-product mission threads, end-to-end integration, and human-centered systems outcomes.
5. Enterprise agents consume scored, traceable capability assertions to form strategic options and systemic risk posture.

Databases index the flow; versioned artifacts remain authoritative.
