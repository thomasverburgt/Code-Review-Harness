# Human Review Gates and Automation Maturity

Human approval is explicit policy, not a missing feature. Begin with human review for all material recommendations and make any reduction in oversight an earned, reversible decision.

| Maturity level | Automation behavior | Minimum evidence for promotion |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Observe | Agents report only; every result is human reviewed. | Contract validity and traceability established. |
| 1 — Assist | Agents draft findings/CAPA and humans disposition them. | Measured agreement and false-positive baseline. |
| 2 — Guarded routing | Low-risk, high-confidence items may be routed automatically; humans approve material actions. | Sustained agreement threshold, low false positives, high reviewability. |
| 3 — Limited auto-promotion | Defined low-risk findings may promote to a tracked backlog under policy. | Approved scope, continuous monitoring, rollback path, periodic audit. |

Example quantitative entry criteria may include at least 95% agreement with designated human reviewers and under 2% false positives over a program-defined representative review population. These are policy examples, not universal thresholds.
