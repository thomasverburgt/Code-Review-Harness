## Adding contributors

A contributor may add their preferred name, GitHub handle, and a concise description of their contribution through a pull request. Inclusion here is optional and does not replace Git attribution, copyright ownership, the Developer Certificate of Origin, or license requirements.

Automated systems and AI tools are not legal contributors and must not add DCO sign-offs. Material AI assistance may be acknowledged in pull-request notes or commit trailers, while the human submitter remains responsible for the contribution.
