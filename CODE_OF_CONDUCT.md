# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participants in Code Review Harness must collaborate professionally, respectfully, and in good faith. The project welcomes constructive participation regardless of background, identity, experience, role, or employer.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- focus criticism on ideas, evidence, implementation, and outcomes;
- explain disagreement clearly and preserve material dissent where architecture or risk remains unresolved;
- accept review without retaliation;
- respect privacy, security constraints, and responsible-disclosure requirements;
- distinguish facts, assumptions, recommendations, and human decisions;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and deliberate disruption.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory remarks, publishing private information, bad-faith manipulation of evidence, impersonation, retaliation, or sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or report conduct to the relevant platform when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and handled privately when practical.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not place sensitive conduct reports in public issues.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant's community-safety principles but is tailored to this project's evidence, governance, and human-authority model.
