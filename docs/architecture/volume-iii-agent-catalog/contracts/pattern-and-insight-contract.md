# Engineering Pattern and Insight Contract

## Pattern candidate

Patterns recognize evidence-backed good practice. Required fields: `pattern_id`, `name`, `scope`, `problem_solved`, `evidence_refs`, `observed_benefits`, `applicability_conditions`, `adoption_risk`, and `promotion_status`. Specialists may nominate; product synthesis may corroborate; capability synthesis may demonstrate repeatability; enterprise governance alone may designate a standard or reference architecture.

## Insight

Insights are neutral, non-actionable observations. Required fields: `insight_id`, `statement`, `evidence_refs`, `confidence`, `possible_consumers`, and `promotion_trigger`. They must not be presented as findings until evidence and assessment support that conversion.
