# Consolidated Human Shadow-Review Handoff

Review all 22 packets as comparison-only candidate material. Reproduce each claim from its exact revision, path, line, excerpt, and hash. Score semantic faithfulness, support level, clarity, scope discipline, and usefulness; request missing evidence explicitly. Do not infer acceptance from schema validity or reviewer consensus.

General engineering interns may review public, sanitized packets for traceability and clarity. Secret-related material remains restricted; risk magnitude, security posture, architectural intent, data-governance meaning, and source authority require qualified adjudication. Reviewer responses never schedule, approve, release, deploy, or accept risk.

After independent responses are retained, qualified adjudicators must preserve disagreements and identify eligible gold or negative examples. The project owner then records one disposition per candidate: revise, continue calibration, candidate-complete, or retained-only.
